Timers persist across page reloads. If you navigate away and come back, each timer picks up where it left off as long as your browser session is active.

On iPhone? iOS requires this site to be added to your Home Screen before notifications work. In Safari, tap the Share icon → "Add to Home Screen" → reopen from your home screen → enable notifications.
